HOW TO EDIT THE NINE STORY SLIDES
=================================

The words on the nine slides live in ONE small file:

    data/story.json

Nothing else needs to be touched.


TO EDIT ON GITHUB
-----------------
1. Open your repository  rutujapublication-in.github.io
2. Click the  data  folder
3. Click  story.json
4. Click the PENCIL icon  at the top right of the file
5. Change any line you want
6. Scroll to the bottom, click  Commit changes
7. Wait 2 minutes, then open the site and press Ctrl+Shift+R

That is all. No other file changes.


WHAT EACH FIELD DOES
--------------------
chapter_mr / chapter_en    the word in the left column. Keep it 1 to 2 words.
narrative_mr / _en         the main text in the middle.
impact_mr / _en            the 2 to 4 words in the highlight zone.
tagline_mr / _en           slide 1 only, instead of narrative.


MARKS YOU CAN USE IN THE NARRATIVE
----------------------------------
  |      a new line, still the same thought
  //     a new block, leaves real space between thoughts
  [[ ]]  a key word, shown in gold
  **     at the START of a line, emphasises that whole line
  >>     at the START of a line, draws it as a chain with arrows

Example:

  "narrative_mr": "राजमाता [[जिजाऊंनी]] शिवबाला घडवलं,|आणि [[शिवाजी महाराजांनी]] पाया रचला।//पुढचा विचार इथे."

  reads as:

      राजमाता जिजाऊंनी शिवबाला घडवलं,        <- जिजाऊंनी in gold
      आणि शिवाजी महाराजांनी पाया रचला।

      ▬
      पुढचा विचार इथे.                        <- a new block


MARKS IN THE IMPACT FIELD
-------------------------
  |      separates the words. The LAST word is shown largest.

  "impact_mr": "मुलींपासून|नवी|सुरुवात"


RULES — PLEASE FOLLOW
---------------------
1. Keep exactly 9 slides. Do not add or remove any.
2. Every [[ needs a matching ]]. An unclosed one shows as plain text.
3. Keep the quotation marks " " around every value.
4. Keep the comma at the end of every line except the last one in a block.
5. Keep impact to 2 to 4 words. More will crowd on a phone.
6. Keep chapter short. Long words shrink the middle column.
7. Slide 1 has tagline, not narrative. That is correct, leave it.


IF SOMETHING BREAKS
-------------------
If the band goes blank after an edit, the file has a typo.
GitHub keeps every version: open story.json, click History,
open the last working version, click the three dots, Revert.


THE TIMING
----------
3 seconds per slide. To change it, open  data/content.json
and edit  "story_seconds": 3


COLOURS AND LOOK — ALSO IN THE SAME FILE
========================================
Every slide carries its own colours. Change a hex code and commit.

  background         the whole slide behind everything
  left_panel         the darker column holding keyword and symbol
  chapter_colour     the keyword text in the left column
  narrative_colour   the ordinary sentences in the middle
  highlight_colour   the [[key words]], impact words and rules
  divider_colour     the line between left column and middle

RULE OF THUMB
  Keep background dark and narrative_colour a pale tint of it,
  so highlight_colour stands out. If narrative_colour is too
  bright it fights the highlight and nothing stands out.

keyword_effect   how the mark under the keyword animates:
  beam   a stroke widening and narrowing
  rings  dots multiplying downward
  door   opening outward
  arrow  travelling across and away
  triad  one, then two, then three
  rise   growing outward from the centre
  spark  expanding from a point
  join   two ends meeting
  glow   a steady halo

symbol           the drawing inside the circle:
  logo    your publication logo (slide 1)
  rings   concentric rings, for legacy
  door    an open door, for access
  arrow   an arrow in a ring, for direction
  triad   three interlocking circles
  base    stacked courses, for foundation
  letter  the letter अ, for beginning
  arc     an arc joining two points
  check   a rising check, for belief

right_divider    true  draws a line between middle and impact zone
                 false separates them by space alone
