RUTUJA — IMAGE AND VIDEO STANDARDS
Follow these for every image and video you ever add.

VIDEO
-----
Upload to YouTube at the HIGHEST resolution your camera produces.
  Shorts / vertical : 1080 x 1920 minimum.  1440 x 2560 or 2160 x 3840 is better.
  Landscape         : 1920 x 1080 minimum.  2560 x 1440 or 3840 x 2160 is better.

YouTube can only ever play back what you uploaded. A 1080p upload can
never be shown at 2K, no matter what the website does. Upload high once
and the site will serve the best the viewer's screen and network allow.

The website always requests the highest quality YouTube will give for the
size of the player, and the player is now as large as the layout permits.

IMAGES
------
Save as WebP. Quality 80. Use squoosh.app - free, no signup.

  Book cover        1200 x 1600   (3:4 portrait)      under 250 KB
  Book inside page  1200 x 1600   (3:4 portrait)      under 250 KB
  Advertisement     1920 x 1080   (16:9 landscape)    under 300 KB
  Experience photo  1600 x 1200   (4:3)               under 300 KB
  News photo        1920 x 1080   (16:9 landscape)    under 300 KB
  Author photo      1000 x 1000   (1:1 square)        under 200 KB
  Logo              1000 x 1000   PNG, transparent    under 150 KB

RETINA - OPTIONAL BUT BETTER
----------------------------
Modern phones have double-density screens. If you also supply a 2x file,
the website uses it automatically on those screens.

  rp-b-001.webp        1200 x 1600   normal screens
  rp-b-001@2x.webp     2400 x 3200   high density screens

Only the normal file is required. If the @2x file is missing the site
simply uses the normal one. Nothing breaks.

NAMING
------
  Book cover      rp-b-001.webp
  Book page       rp-b-001-p1.webp
  Advertisement   rp-ad-001.webp
  Experience      rp-e-001.webp
  News            rp-n-001.webp
  Author          rp-a-001.webp

Lowercase only. Hyphens, not spaces. No Marathi in file names.

WHERE THEY GO
-------------
  assets/img/books/          book covers and inside pages
  assets/img/ads/            advertisements
  assets/img/experiences/    school, teacher, student, retailer photos
  assets/img/news/           news and event photos

WHY SIZE LIMITS MATTER
----------------------
Most of your visitors are on village 4G. A 4 MB photo from a phone camera
takes about 20 seconds to appear. Compressed to 250 KB it takes under one
second and looks identical on a screen. Compressing is not a compromise in
quality - it removes data the eye cannot see.
